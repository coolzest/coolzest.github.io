    Unit 06 牧场¶

    The Pasture¶

    原文链接：Part 1 / Part 2

    复习方式：先读英文，再看译文，最后只背本句下面的词和短语。

    001

    Night settled, thick with the acrid odors of gunpowder and blood.

    夜幕降临，充满了火药和血的刺鼻气味。

    settled /ˈsɛtəld/ 定居 gunpowder /ˈgənˌpaʊdər/ 火药

    002

    No moon meant firing at muzzle flashes, an inexact method by which to combat one's foe, but the enemy had no advantage either.

    没有月亮意味着用枪口闪光开火，这是一种不精确的打击敌人的方法，但敌人也没有优势。

    moon /mun/ 月亮 flashes /ˈflæʃɪz/ 闪烁 inexact /ˌɪnɪgˈzækt/ 不精确的 foe /foʊ/ 敌人 advantage /ædˈvæntɪʤ/ 优势

    003

    With artillery fire blanketing the field of battle, Jack had no opportunity to go to Mike's aid.

    由于炮火覆盖了战场，杰克没有机会去援助迈克。

    artillery /ɑrˈtɪləri/ 炮兵 blanketing /ˈblæŋkətɪŋ/ 覆盖 opportunity /ˌɑpərˈtunəti/ 机会

    004

    He poked his head over the rim of the trench to see if he could spot Mike's corpse.

    他把头伸出战壕的边缘，看看是否能发现迈克的尸体。

    005

    He knew he was dead.

    他知道他已经死了。

    006

    He had heard his scream of pain when he was hit.

    当他被击中时，他听到了痛苦的尖叫声。

    007

    His agonized moans had grown weaker and less frequent and finally ceased about ten minutes ago.

    他痛苦的呻吟声越来越弱，频率也越来越低，大约十分钟前终于停止了。

    agonized /ˈægəˌnaɪzd/ 痛苦的 frequent /ˈfrikˌwɛnt/ 经常 finally /ˈfaɪnəli/ 最后 minutes /ˈmɪnəts/ 分钟

    008

    The regiment had been under heavy bombardment for two days, without respite.

    该团已经连续两天遭受猛烈炮击，没有喘息的机会。

    regiment /ˈrɛʤəmənt/ 团 bombardment /bɑmˈbɑrdmənt/ 轰击 respite /ˈrɛspɪt/ 喘息

    009

    He had been too late to stop Mike as he propelled himself out of the foxhole and staggered as he hit the muddy terrain.

    当迈克从散兵坑里爬出来时，他来不及阻止，当他跌倒在泥泞的地面上时，他摇摇晃晃。

    propelled /prəˈpɛld/ 推动的 foxhole /ˈfɑksˌhoʊl/ 散兵坑 staggered /ˈstægərd/ 交错 terrain /təreɪn/ 地形

    010

    In hindsight, he should have seen it coming.

    事后看来，他应该预见到这一点。

    hindsight /ˈhaɪnˌsaɪt/ 事后诸葛亮

    011

    Mike had started to break yesterday.

    迈克昨天就开始崩溃了。

    started /ˈstɑrtɪd/ 开始了

    012

    He was becoming bizarre, talking about his patriotic duty to expel the enemy from these shores.

    他变得很奇怪，谈论他将敌人驱逐出这些海岸的爱国义务。

    becoming /bɪˈkəmɪŋ/ 成为 bizarre /bɪˈzɑr/ 奇异的 talking /ˈtɔkɪŋ/ 说 patriotic /ˌpeɪtriˈɑtɪk/ 爱国

    013

    Paradoxically, just before he hopped over the edge of the trench he scorned the day he had heeded the call to enlist.

    矛盾的是，就在他跳出战壕边缘之前，他对自己响应入伍号召的那一天嗤之以鼻。

    paradoxically /ˌpɛrəˈdɑksəkəli/ 自相矛盾地 scorned /skɔrnd/ 被蔑视

    014

    It pained him to realize that his one friend in this ungodly war had betrayed him by dying.

    当他意识到他在这场不敬虔的战争中的一位朋友因死亡而背叛了他时，他感到痛苦。

    realize /ˈriəˌlaɪz/ 意识到 ungodly /ənˈgɔdli/ 不敬虔的 war /wɔr/ 战争 betrayed /bɪˈtreɪd/ 被背叛

    015

    Soldiers don't often make friends with one another, knowing they might die.

    士兵们知道自己可能会死去，因此不会经常互相交朋友。

    soldiers /ˈsoʊlʤərz/ 士兵 friends /frɛndz/ 朋友们 knowing /noʊɪŋ/ 会心

    016

    He and Mike were different.

    他和迈克不同。

    different /ˈdɪfərənt/ 不同的

    017

    They had known each other since they were children.

    他们从小就认识。

    children /ˈʧɪldrən/ 孩子们

    018

    Mike was the best man when Jack and Lily walked down the aisle just a week before they left the dock and crossed the channel to engage the enemy.

    就在杰克和莉莉离开码头、越过英吉利海峡与敌人交战前一周，当杰克和莉莉走上过道时，迈克是伴郎。

    aisle /aɪəl/ 走道 crossed /krɔst/ 交叉的 channel /ˈʧænəl/ 渠道

    019

    Clouds tumbled and overlapped one another as the wind began to freshen.

    随着风开始变得清新，云层翻滚并相互重叠。

    tumbled /ˈtəmbəld/ 跌倒了 overlapped /ˈoʊvərˌlæpt/ 重叠的 freshen /ˈfrɛʃən/ 清新

    020

    It was getting colder now.

    现在天气越来越冷了。

    021

    The prospect of another night of rain, or perhaps even snow, made Jack quiver with dread.

    一想到又会下一场雨，甚至可能下雪，杰克就害怕得浑身发抖。

    prospect /ˈprɑspɛkt/ 前景 perhaps /pərˈhæps/ 也许

    022

    Blood tinged water was beginning to crust over with ice.

    带有血色的水开始结冰。

    beginning /bɪˈgɪnɪŋ/ 开始

    023

    He could feel his toes and fingers stiffen as the temperature dropped.

    随着温度的下降，他能感觉到自己的脚趾和手指都变得僵硬了。

    fingers /ˈfɪŋgərz/ 手指 stiffen /ˈstɪfən/ 变硬 temperature /ˈtɛmpərəʧər/ 温度 dropped /drɑpt/ 掉落

    024

    Maybe Mike was better off than he.

    也许迈克比他过得更好。

    025

    At least the cold wouldn't bother him any more.

    至少寒冷不会再困扰他了。

    wouldn't /ˈwʊdənt/ 不会

    026

    Suddenly, comets of light began to streak across the night sky.

    突然，夜空开始划过彗星。

    suddenly /ˈsədənli/ 突然

    027

    They were using flares!

    他们使用的是照明弹！

    028

    The increased tempo of cannon fire coming from the left flank, shattering the earth around them, could only mean the enemy had sensed their weakness, and was coming in for the kill.

    从左翼射出的炮火速度越来越快，粉碎了周围的大地，这只能意味着敌人已经察觉到了他们的弱点，正在展开杀戮。

    increased /ˌɪnˈkrist/ 增加 shattering /ˈʃætərɪŋ/ 破碎的 weakness /ˈwiknəs/ 弱点

    029

    They were heading straight for the underbelly!

    他们直奔腹地而去！

    heading /ˈhɛdɪŋ/ 标题 straight /streɪt/ 直的 underbelly /ˈəndərˌbɛli/ 软肋

    030

    Jack's rifle misfired.

    杰克的步枪失火了。

    misfired /mɪsˈfaɪər/ 失火

    031

    The whole corps had been issued new guns a few days before this campaign had started.

    在这场战役开始前几天，全军都配发了新枪。

    campaign /kæmˈpeɪn/ 活动 started /ˈstɑrtɪd/ 开始了

    032

    It wasn't long before they discovered that the alloy used in the barrels couldn't endure prolonged firing, causing the shell casings to adhere in the bore.

    不久之后，他们发现枪管中使用的合金无法承受长时间的射击，导致弹壳粘附在枪膛中。

    discovered /dɪˈskəvərd/ 发现了 barrels /ˈbɛrəlz/ 桶 couldn't /ˈkʊdənt/ 不能 prolonged /prəˈlɔŋd/ 延长 causing /ˈkɔzɪŋ/ 导致

    033

    How could he repel the enemy without a weapon, Jack felt a stab of pain in his right thigh.

    没有武器怎么击退敌人，杰克感到右大腿一阵刺痛。

    034

    The ground around him ruptured.

    他周围的地面破裂了。

    ruptured /ˈrəpʧərd/ 破裂的

    035

    Jerking from side to side, he tried to dodge falling rocks and the clods of dirt raining down upon him.

    他左右晃动，试图躲避掉落的岩石和雨点般落在他身上的土块。

    jerking /ˈʤərkɪŋ/ 抽搐 falling /ˈfɑlɪŋ/ 坠落 raining /ˈreɪnɪŋ/ 下雨

    036

    He slumped into a prone posture as he felt himself shoved from behind by an unknown force that felt as if it might have been a racing locomotive.

    他瘫倒在地，感觉自己被一股未知的力量从后面推了过来，感觉好像是一辆赛车机车。

    slumped /sləmpt/ 颓然地 posture /ˈpɑsʧər/ 姿势 unknown /ənˈnoʊn/ 未知 locomotive /ˌloʊkəˈmoʊtɪv/ 机车

    037

    The last thing he heard, before his world was overturned, was a chorus of screams. “Am I dead, Where am I, Where is everybody,” Cognitively, Jack realized he was still functioning physiologically so that meant he was still alive, but for how long, He couldn't move.

    在他的世界被颠覆之前，他听到的最后一件事是齐声尖叫。 “我死了吗？我在哪里，每个人都在哪里？”从认知上来说，杰克意识到他的生理功能仍然正常，这意味着他还活着，但是多久了，他无法动弹。

    overturned /ˈoʊvərˌtərnd/ 推翻了 screams /skrimz/ 尖叫声 everybody /ˈɛvriˌbɑdi/ 大家 cognitively：认知上的 realized /ˈriəˌlaɪzd/ 实现了

    038

    Maybe the impact had fractured his spine and he was paralyzed.

    也许撞击使他的脊椎骨折，导致他瘫痪了。

    fractured /ˈfrækʧərd/ 骨折的 paralyzed /ˈpɛrəˌlaɪzd/ 瘫痪的

    039

    He tried，analytically，to assess the damage incurred by moving his limbs, one by one, in a clockwise direction, starting with his right arm.

    他试图通过分析来评估从右臂开始顺时针方向一一移动四肢所造成的损伤。

    analytically /ˌænəˈlɪtɪkli/ 分析地 incurred /ˌɪnˈkərd/ 发生 clockwise /ˈklɑkˌwaɪz/ 顺时针 direction /dɪˈrɛkʃɪn/ 方向 starting /ˈstɑrtɪŋ/ 开始

    040

    Everything appeared able to be mobilized but for some reason he couldn't get up off the ground.

    一切似乎都可以动员起来，但不知为何他却无法从地上站起来。

    everything /ˈɛvriˌθɪŋ/ 一切 appeared /əˈpɪrd/ 出现了 mobilized /ˈmoʊbəˌlaɪzd/ 动员起来 couldn't /ˈkʊdənt/ 不能

    041

    Everything went black again.

    一切又变黑了。

    everything /ˈɛvriˌθɪŋ/ 一切

    042

    Jack led his horse by the reins as they stepped onto the overpass bridging the gap between the plateaus.

    杰克牵着马，踏上连接高原之间的立交桥。

    stepped /stɛpt/ 阶梯式 overpass /ˈoʊvərˌpæs/ 立交桥 bridging /ˈbrɪʤɪŋ/ 桥接 plateaus /plæˈtoʊ/ 高原

    043

    In the distance, he could see a twelve point buck grazing in the pasture, silhouetted against the waning sunset.

    远处，他可以看到一头十二点雄鹿在牧场上吃草，在夕阳的余晖映衬下，它的轮廓清晰可见。

    distance /ˈdɪstəns/ 距离 grazing /ˈgreɪzɪŋ/ 放牧 pasture /ˈpæsʧər/ 牧场 silhouetted /ˌsɪləˈwɛtɪd/ 剪影

    044

    Out of nowhere, a bull came charging across the meadow toward him.

    一头公牛不知从哪里冒出来，穿过草地向他冲过来。

    nowhere /ˈnoʊˌwɛr/ 无处 charging /ˈʧɑrʤɪŋ/ 收费

    045

    He tried to run but his feet were pegged to the bridge.

    他试图逃跑，但双脚被钉在桥上。

    046

    Drifting in and out of consciousness, Jack tried to make sense of what had happened to him.

    杰克时而意识清醒，时而清醒，试图弄清楚发生在他身上的事情。

    drifting /ˈdrɪftɪŋ/ 漂流 consciousness /ˈkɑnʃəsnəs/ 意识 happened /ˈhæpənd/ 发生了

    047

    He seemed to be in a cavity under the ground.

    他似乎在地底下的一个洞里。

    048

    The earth was compacted on his legs but he thought he might be able to rotate his body enough to make himself a little more comfortable.

    泥土压在他的腿上，但他想他也许可以旋转自己的身体，让自己舒服一点。

    compacted /kəmˈpæktɪd/ 压实的 thought /θɔt/ 想法 comfortable /ˈkəmfərtəbəl/ 舒服的

    049

    Was that a shaft of light he could see through the groove between the fingers of his left hand covering his eyes, He groped to clear a tiny bit more space for himself.

    那是他从遮住眼睛的左手手指间的凹槽里看到的一道光柱吗？他摸索着为自己腾出一点空间。

    fingers /ˈfɪŋgərz/ 手指 covering /ˈkəvərɪŋ/ 覆盖

    050

    The shutters of his consciousness closed again.

    他意识的百叶窗再次关闭。

    shutters /ˈʃətərz/ 百叶窗 consciousness /ˈkɑnʃəsnəs/ 意识

    051

    The galaxy was being probed by lunar modules shuttling between Earth and the other planets.

    穿梭于地球和其他行星之间的登月舱正在对银河系进行探测。

    modules /ˈmɑʤulz/ 模块 shuttling /ˈʃətəlɪŋ/ 穿梭 planets /ˈplænəts/ 行星

    052

    As a member of a federation of geologists, Jack and his team had to follow the seam of ore to its source, a pasture on a distant asteroid.

    作为地质学家联合会的成员，杰克和他的团队必须沿着矿层找到它的源头，即遥远小行星上的牧场。

    federation /ˌfɛdərˈeɪʃən/ 联邦 geologists /ʤiˈɑləʤɪsts/ 地质学家 pasture /ˈpæsʧər/ 牧场 distant /ˈdɪstənt/ 遥远 asteroid /ˈæstərˌɔɪd/ 小行星

    053
