# 👑 6级作文通用模板

## 一、 第一段：开篇引入（第 1-2 句）

### 🚀 节点 1：第一句（根据题型三选一）

* **【开放话题类】**
  > `[动名词短语/核心话题词]` has sparked heated discussions, as it points to a vital  **(social issue / employment trend / core virtue)** .
  >
* **【名言哲理类】**
  > The saying **“`[抄写名言原句]`”** has sparked heated discussions, as it reflects a vital  **(way of thinking / spirit / attitude / core virtue)** .
  >
* **【图表图画类】**
  > As is vividly depicted in the  **(picture / graph)** , `[一句话描述图画主体]`. This **(impressive scene / sticky situation)** has sparked heated discussions, as it points to a vital  **(technology trend / social issue)** .
  >

### 🚀 节点 2：第二句定性（根据态度二选一）

* **【积极 / 中性话题】**
  > What lies at the heart of this **(saying / quality / choice)** is not merely a passing  **(idea / trend)** , but a topic closely linked to individual growth and social progress.
  >
* **【消极 / 反思话题】**
  > What lies at the heart of this **(situation / issue)** is not merely a passing  **(fashion / challenge)** , but a topic closely linked to individual growth and social progress.
  >

## 二、 第二段：多维论证（第 3-6 句）

### 🚀 节点 3：承上启下（根据态度二选一）

* **【积极 / 中性话题】**
  > Two main reasons can explain the importance of this issue.
  >
* **【消极 / 反思话题】**
  > Two main reasons can account for the gravity of this situation.
  >

### 🚀 节点 4：个人层面论点

> First and foremost, `[同义改写后的核心行为]` acts as a powerful catalyst for  **`(career success / professional recognition / intellectual growth / physical and mental well-being / information security)`** . *(←根据题意在括号中稳妥挑选一个结果词)*

### 🚀 节点 5：因果解释（根据态度二选一，整句直接插入）

* **【若选积极/中性（终极通用插件一）】**
  > Confronted with the rapid development of modern society, individuals who attach great importance to this issue are more likely to adapt to future changes and achieve well-rounded development.
  >
* **【若选消极/反思（终极通用插件二）】**
  > Confronted with the deep anxiety in contemporary life, individuals who blindly indulge in this trend are more likely to lose their sense of direction and fall into a psychological dilemma.
  >

### 🚀 节点 6：社会层面论点（根据态度二选一）

* **【积极 / 中性话题】**
  > Moreover, the implications of this **(virtue / choice / technology)** extend far beyond personal  **(growth / achievement)** ; it also injects vitality into social progress. **Fostering a culture of `[核心正面品质，如：social responsibility / critical thinking / continuous innovation / rational career choices]` lays a solid foundation for sustainable development.**
  >
* **【消极 / 反思话题】**
  > Moreover, the implications of this **(issue / habit)** extend far beyond personal loss; it also poses a serious threat to social harmony. **Fostering a culture of `[核心防范意识，如：network safety / mutual trust]` lays a solid foundation for sustainable development.**
  >

## 三、 第三段：总结升华（第 7-9 句）

### 🚀 节点 7：总结段第一句（根据态度二选一）

* **【积极 / 中性话题】**
  > In light of the analysis above, it is high time we recognized the far-reaching implications of **(embracing this virtue / adopting this approach)** properly.
  >
* **【消极 / 反思话题】**
  > In light of the analysis above, it is high time we recognized the far-reaching implications of **(curbing this problem / correcting this habit)** effectively.
  >

### 🚀 节点 8：呼吁行动（根据态度二选一）

* **【积极 / 中性话题】**
  > To fully harness its potential, joint efforts from society and individuals are needed.
  >
* **【消极 / 反思话题】**
  > To fundamentally eliminate its hazards, joint efforts from society and individuals are needed.
  >

### 🚀 节点 9：倒装收尾句（根据态度二选一，整句直接插入）

* **【若选积极/中性（终极通用插件一）】**
  > Only in this way can we broaden our horizons, unleash our hidden potential, and embrace a more promising future.
  >
* **【若选消极/反思（终极通用插件二）】**
  > Only in this way can we eliminate these hidden hazards, restore rational thinking, and maintain the long-term well-being of our community.
  >
