    Unit 08 中美关系史（一）¶

    Chinese-American Relations: A History(Ⅰ)¶

    原文链接：Part 1 / Part 2

    复习方式：先读英文，再看译文，最后只背本句下面的词和短语。

    001

    The Nineteenth Century to World WarⅡ The Nineteenth Century In the 19th century, the United States was a relative newcomer to the area of international affairs.

    19世纪到第二次世界大战 19世纪 19世纪，美国在国际事务领域相对较新。

    nineteenth /ˈnaɪnˈtinθ/ 第十九 century /ˈsɛnʧəri/ 世纪 war /wɔr/ 战争 relative /ˈrɛlətɪv/ 相对的 newcomer /ˈnuˌkəmər/ 新人

    002

    Relations with China really began, not so subtly, in the 19th century with its discriminatory immigration policy against China.

    与中国的关系真正开始，并不那么微妙，是在19世纪，当时中国对中国实行歧视性移民政策。

    relations /riˈleɪʃənz/ 关系 century /ˈsɛnʧəri/ 世纪 discriminatory /dɪˈskrɪmənəˌtɔri/ 歧视性的 immigration /ˌɪməˈgreɪʃən/ 移民

    003

    The Gold Rush of 1849 in California, the building of railroads, and the American industrial revolution of the second half of the 19th century, attracted many Chinese immigrants with dreams of the good life in America.

    1849年加州的淘金热、铁路的修建以及19世纪下半叶美国的工业革命，吸引了许多怀揣着美国美好生活梦想的华人移民。

    california /ˌkæləˈfɔrnjə/ 加利福尼亚州 building /ˈbɪldɪŋ/ 建筑 railroads /ˈreɪˌlroʊdz/ 铁路 industrial /ˌɪnˈdəstriəl/ 工业的 revolution /ˌrɛvəˈluʃən/ 革命

    004

    At that time, it was perceived by most of the world, that America was the land of opportunity, success, and wealth.

    当时，世界上大多数人都认为美国是一片充满机遇、成功和财富的土地。

    perceived /pərˈsivd/ 感知到的 opportunity /ˌɑpərˈtunəti/ 机会 success /səkˈsɛs/ 成功

    005

    As the Chinese population in the United States grew in size, pressures to limit the number of these coming into the United States became strong.

    随着美国华人人口数量的增长，限制华人进入美国的压力也越来越大。

    population /ˌpɑpjəˈleɪʃən/ 人口 pressures /ˈprɛʃərz/ 压力

    006

    Laws, such as placing a police tax on Chinese people in California in 1862 and The Chinese Exclusion Act passed in 1882, officially testified to blatant discrimination against Chinese people.

    1862年加州向华人征收警察税、1882年通过的《排华法案》等法律，正式证明了对华人的公然歧视。

    placing /ˈpleɪsɪŋ/ 放置 tax /tæks/ 税 california /ˌkæləˈfɔrnjə/ 加利福尼亚州 exclusion /ɪkˈskluʒən/ 排除 officially /əˈfɪʃəli/ 正式

    007

    The latter felt forced to congregate in areas of big cities, such as San Francisco, New York, and Boston.

    后者感到被迫聚集在旧金山、纽约和波士顿等大城市地区。

    congregate /ˈkɑŋgrəˌgeɪt/ 聚集 francisco /frænˈsɪskoʊ/ 弗朗西斯科

    008

    Chinatown soon became part of American urban vocabulary.

    唐人街很快就成为美国城市词汇的一部分。

    chinatown /ˈʧaɪnəˌtaʊn/ 唐人街 vocabulary /voʊˈkæbjəˌlɛri/ 词汇

    009

    It seemed that the timid Chinese were susceptible to being pushed around.

    看来胆怯的中国人很容易被人摆布。

    susceptible /səˈsɛptəbəl/ 易受影响的

    010

    It appeared that Chinese and other Oriental immigrants were not welcome with open arms, but were welcome only when hard labour was needed to do the toughest jobs, especially in railroad construction and in the new industries that were fast developing at the time.

    看来中国和其他东方移民并没有受到张开双臂的欢迎，而是只有在需要艰苦劳动从事最艰苦的工作时才受到欢迎，特别是在铁路建设和当时快速发展的新兴产业中。

    appeared /əˈpɪrd/ 出现了 oriental /ˌɔriˈɛnəl/ 东方的 immigrants /ˈɪməgrənts/ 移民 welcome /ˈwɛlkəm/ 欢迎 toughest /ˈtəfəst/ 最难的

    011

    It would be well into the 20th century before such discriminatory laws would be suspended.

    直到 20 世纪，此类歧视性法律才会被暂停。

    century /ˈsɛnʧəri/ 世纪 discriminatory /dɪˈskrɪmənəˌtɔri/ 歧视性的 suspended /səˈspɛndɪd/ 暂停

    012

    The Early Twentieth Century During the second half of the 19th century, the United States was preoccupied with a civil war and a post civil war industrial revolution.

    二十世纪初 十九世纪下半叶，美国正忙于内战和内战后的工业革命。

    twentieth /tˈwɛnˌtiɛθ/ 第二十 century /ˈsɛnʧəri/ 世纪 preoccupied /priˈɑkjəˌpaɪd/ 心事重重 war /wɔr/ 战争 industrial /ˌɪnˈdəstriəl/ 工业的

    013

    American foreign policy with China did not really take form until 1899 and 1900.

    美国对华外交政策直到1899年和1900年才真正形成。

    foreign /ˈfɔrən/ 外国的

    014

    By the turn of the century, the United States was ascending as a major player in international affairs, especially in the western hemisphere.

    到了世纪之交，美国逐渐成为国际事务中的主要参与者，特别是在西半球。

    century /ˈsɛnʧəri/ 世纪 ascending /əˈsɛndɪŋ/ 上升 international /ˌɪnərˈnæʃənɑl/ 国际的 affairs /əˈfɛrz/ 事务 especially /əˈspɛʃəli/ 尤其

    015

    American foreign policy, at the time, focused mostly on Latin America.

    当时美国的外交政策主要集中在拉丁美洲。

    foreign /ˈfɔrən/ 外国的 focused /ˈfoʊkɪst/ 专注的

    016

    However, in 1899, the Americans saw economic opportunities in an already politically suppressed China.

    然而，1899年，美国人在政治上受到压制的中国看到了经济机会。

    however /ˌhaʊˈɛvər/ 然而 americans /əˈmɛrɪkənz/ 美国人 economic /ˌɛkəˈnɑmɪk/ 经济的 opportunities /ˌɑpərˈtunətiz/ 机会 already /ɔˈrɛdi/ 已经

    017

    For decades, European countries had been reaping the economic benefits by exploiting of the country's resources and markets while claiming chunks of territory as their own.

    几十年来，欧洲国家一直通过开发本国的资源和市场来获取经济利益，同时声称拥有大片领土。

    decades /ˈdɛkeɪdz/ 几十年 european /ˌjʊrəˈpiən/ 欧洲的 countries /ˈkəntriz/ 国家 reaping /ˈripɪŋ/ 收获 economic /ˌɛkəˈnɑmɪk/ 经济的

    018

    It had become a closed club of the countries already established there.

    它已成为已在那里建立的国家的封闭俱乐部。

    countries /ˈkəntriz/ 国家 already /ɔˈrɛdi/ 已经 established /ɪˈstæblɪʃt/ 已确立的

    019

    The United States, fearing that China was about to officially partitioned, wanted access to those lucrative assets as well.

    美国担心中国即将正式分裂，也希望获得这些利润丰厚的资产。

    fearing /ˈfɪrɪŋ/ 害怕 officially /əˈfɪʃəli/ 正式 partitioned /pɑrˈtɪʃənd/ 分区的 lucrative /ˈlukrətɪv/ 有利可图的

    020

    American Secretary of State, John Hay, perhaps using some Big Stick and gunboat tactics, popular American strategies at the time, was well positioned to get the established foreign nations in China to conform to an agreement called the Open Door policy for China.

    美国国务卿约翰·海伊也许使用了当时流行的美国策略——大棒和炮舰策略，处于有利地位，可以让在中国的老牌外国遵守一项名为“对华门户开放政策”的协议。

    secretary /ˈsɛkrəˌtɛri/ 秘书 perhaps /pərˈhæps/ 也许 gunboat /ˈgənˌboʊt/ 炮舰 tactics /ˈtæktɪks/ 策略 popular /ˈpɑpjələr/ 受欢迎的

    021

    This benchmark intervention by the United States, conferred on all countries, equal and impartial trade with all parts of China, while preserving the territorial and administrative integrity of the country.

    美国的这一基准干预，赋予所有国家与中国各地平等、公正的贸易，同时维护国家的领土和行政完整。

    benchmark /ˈbɛnʧˌmɑrk/ 基准 intervention /ˌɪntərˈvɛnʃən/ 干涉 conferred /kənˈfərd/ 授予 countries /ˈkəntriz/ 国家 impartial /ˌɪmˈpɑrʃəl/ 公正

    022

    The American approach did little to respect China's customary opposition to foreign intrusion.

    美国的做法几乎没有尊重中国反对外国入侵的一贯做法。

    approach /əˈproʊʧ/ 方法 respect /rɪˈspɛkt/ 尊重 customary /ˈkəstəˌmɛri/ 习惯的 opposition /ˌɑpəˈzɪʃən/ 反对 foreign /ˈfɔrən/ 外国的

    023

    To China, the United States was only one more country to bully it, to exploit its resources and sovereignty and, further, to deny it of its autonomy, integrity, and dignity.

    对中国来说，美国只是又一个欺凌它、剥削它的资源和主权、进一步剥夺它的自治、完整和尊严的国家。

    country /ˈkəntri/ 国家 exploit /ˌɛkˈsplɔɪt/ 开发 resources /ˈrisɔrsɪz/ 资源 sovereignty /ˈsɑvrənti/ 主权 further /ˈfərðər/ 更远

    024

    This collective foreign presence, boosted by American interests, diffused any hope for China to break the chains of humiliating foreign occupation.

    在美国利益的推动下，这种集体的外国存在消除了中国打破侮辱性外国占领锁链的希望。

    collective /kəˈlɛktɪv/ 集体 foreign /ˈfɔrən/ 外国的 presence /ˈprɛzəns/ 在场 boosted /ˈbustɪd/ 增强的 interests /ˈɪntərɪsts/ 兴趣

    025

    The Chinese were virtually captives or prisoners in their own country.

    中国人实际上是自己国家的俘虏或囚犯。

    virtually /ˈvərʧuəli/ 几乎 captives /ˈkæptɪvz/ 俘虏 prisoners /ˈprɪzənərz/ 囚犯 country /ˈkəntri/ 国家

    026

    The United States did not deviate far from this economic policy toward China, until the communist take over in 1949.

    直到1949年共产党上台之前，美国并没有偏离这一对华经济政策。

    deviate /ˈdiviˌeɪt/ 偏离 economic /ˌɛkəˈnɑmɪk/ 经济的 communist /ˈkɑmjənəst/ 共产

    027

    One could only imagine how the Chinese must have felt at the turn of the century.

    人们只能想象中国人在世纪之交的感受。

    imagine /ˌɪˈmæʤən/ 想象 century /ˈsɛnʧəri/ 世纪

    028

    Could any American imagine a scenario of the shoe being on the other foot (putting themselves in the place of the Chinese), How would Americans have felt if Chinese gunboats patrolled the Mississippi River up to St. Louis, a major city in the heart of America, What if the Chinese could come and go anywhere in the United States, being completely immune to all American laws.

    任何一个美国人都能想象一下换句话说（把自己放在中国人的位置上）吗？如果中国的炮艇在密西西比河上巡逻到美国心脏地带的主要城市圣路易斯，美国人会有何感受？如果中国人可以在美国任何地方来往，完全不受所有美国法律的影响，会怎样？

    imagine /ˌɪˈmæʤən/ 想象 scenario /sɪˈnɛrioʊ/ 设想 putting /ˈpʊtɪŋ/ 推杆 americans /əˈmɛrɪkənz/ 美国人 gunboats /ˈgənˌboʊts/ 炮艇

    029

    Could Americans accept Chinatown in Boston of San Francisco being under Chinese law, and displaying signs with such captions as “No Americans or dogs allowed”, What if Manhattan Island and California were annexed by China, Would Americans tolerate their own officials being in collusion with, and being bribed by Chinese authorities, to let all of above to take place, One would think not.

    美国人能否接受旧金山波士顿唐人街受中国法律管辖，并悬挂“美国人和狗不得入内”等标语，如果曼哈顿岛和加利福尼亚州被中国吞并怎么办，美国人是否会容忍自己的官员与中国当局勾结并被中国当局贿赂，让上述一切发生，人们认为不会。

    take place：发生；举行 americans /əˈmɛrɪkənz/ 美国人 chinatown /ˈʧaɪnəˌtaʊn/ 唐人街 francisco /frænˈsɪskoʊ/ 弗朗西斯科 law /lɔ/ 法律 displaying /dɪˈspleɪɪŋ/ 显示

    030

    Is it any wonder that imperialism had become such an abominable term to the Chinese people, The Chinese have made some conscientious attempts to fight back, in efforts to defy foreign presence in China, but without much support during the slack reign of the Qing Emperor and the Dowager Empress.

    难怪帝国主义对中国人民来说已经成为一个如此令人憎恶的词了。中国人曾做出过一些认真的反击尝试，试图反抗外国势力在中国的存在，但在清朝皇帝和太后统治的衰弱时期却没有得到多少支持。

    imperialism /ˌɪmˈpɪriəˌlɪzəm/ 帝国主义 abominable /əˈbɑmənəbəl/ 可恶 conscientious /ˌkɑnʃiˈɛnʃəs/ 尽责的 attempts /əˈtɛmpts/ 尝试 efforts /ˈɛfərts/ 努力

    031

    The Imperial family, for so long, had been extremely selfindulgent.

    长期以来，皇室极其放纵。

    imperial /ˌɪmˈpɪriəl/ 帝国的 extremely /ɪkˈstrimli/ 极其 indulgent /ˌɪnˈdəlʤənt/ 放纵的

    032

    It paid little attention to the realities of what was happening inside China.

    它很少关注中国国内正在发生的现实。

    attention /əˈtɛnʃən/ 注意力 realities /ˌriˈæləˌtiz/ 现实 happening /ˈhæpənɪŋ/ 正在发生

    033

    For so long, the nation's policies revolved around the whims of the Imperial Family.

    长期以来，国家的政策都是围绕皇室的一时兴起而制定的。

    policies /ˈpɑləsiz/ 政策 revolved /riˈvɑlvd/ 旋转的 imperial /ˌɪmˈpɪriəl/ 帝国的

    034

    With reliance on its own resources, a secret society, called the Order of Literary Patriotic Harmonious Fists, made one last desperate attempt at revenge, to rid the country of foreigners.

    一个名为“文学爱国和谐拳会”的秘密社团依靠自己的资源，进行了最后一次绝望的复仇，以清除这个国家的外国人。
