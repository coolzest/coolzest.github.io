    Unit 01 另一个世界¶

    In Another World¶

    原文链接：Part 1 / Part 2

    复习方式：先读英文，再看译文，最后只背本句下面的词和短语。

    001

    Here I am, in China, half way around the world from home.

    我在这里，在距离家乡半个地球之外的中国。

    half way around the world：相隔半个地球；离得非常远

    002

    As I look at my clock and calculate the time back home I realize that half a world away, people are busy getting ready for a wedding.

    当我看着时钟计算回家的时间时，我意识到在半个地球之外，人们正在忙着准备婚礼。

    calculate /ˈkælkjəˌleɪt/ 计算 realize /ˈriəˌlaɪz/ 意识到 wedding /ˈwɛdɪŋ/ 婚礼

    003

    The bride is my niece, a person I first met when she was three days old.

    新娘是我的侄女，我第一次见到她时她才三天。

    bride /braɪd/ 新娘 niece /nis/ 侄女

    004

    I think back to the first time I had the opportunity to become acquainted with my niece.

    我回想起第一次有机会认识我的侄女。

    think back to：回想起 become acquainted with：开始认识；熟悉 opportunity /ˌɑpərˈtunəti/ 机会 niece /nis/ 侄女

    005

    As my sister cradled her in her arms, the infant girl clasped her mother's outstretched finger.

    当我姐姐将她抱在怀里时，女婴紧握着母亲伸出的手指。

    cradled /ˈkreɪdəl/ v. 轻轻抱着；把……放在摇篮里 clasped /klæspt/ 紧握着 outstretched /aʊtˈstrɛʧt/ 伸展的

    006

    My intuition told me that her temperament would be a sunny disposition, a joy to people around her.

    我的直觉告诉我，她的性格会是阳光的性格，给周围的人带来欢乐。

    intuition /ˌɪntuˈɪʃən/ 直觉 temperament /ˈtɛmpərmənt/ 气质 disposition /ˌdɪspəˈzɪʃən/ 性格

    007

    As she grew, the cute little girl had an infectious laugh that started as a small grin, then became a giggle and grew louder and louder until it triggered a response from all the people around her and they joined in.

    随着她的成长，这个可爱的小女孩的笑声很有感染力，一开始只是小笑，然后变成咯咯笑，声音越来越大，直到引起周围所有人的反应，他们也加入进来。

    infectious /ˌɪnˈfɛkʃəs/ 传染性的 started /ˈstɑrtɪd/ 开始了 triggered /ˈtrɪgərd/ 触发的 response /rɪˈspɑns/ 回复

    008

    As an adolescent, she studied hard and enjoyed well-deserved success in secondary school as she interacted with her peer group.

    青少年时期，她努力学习，在中学时与同龄人交往，取得了当之无愧的成功。

    adolescent /ˌædəˈlɛsənt/ 青少年 studied /ˈstədid/ v. 学习；研究 enjoyed /ˌɛnˈʤɔɪd/ 很享受 well-deserved /dɪˈzərvd/ 当之无愧 success /səkˈsɛs/ 成功

    009

    After receiving her high school diploma, she chose nursing as her career.

    获得高中文凭后，她选择了护理作为她的职业。

    receiving /rɪˈsivɪŋ/ v. 收到；获得 diploma /dɪˈploʊmɑ/ 文凭 nursing /ˈnərsɪŋ/ 护理

    010

    From the beginning of the young couples romance it was evident that they were compatible and were completely intrigued with each other.

    从这对年轻情侣的浪漫开始，就可以看出他们很合得来，并且彼此完全感兴趣。

    beginning /bɪˈgɪnɪŋ/ 开始 couples /ˈkəpəlz/ 情侣 romance /ˈroʊmæns/ 浪漫 evident /ˈɛvədənt/ 明显 compatible /kəmˈpætəbəl/ 兼容的

    011

    The couple had an engagement party when I was at home in Canada a few months ago.

    几个月前，当我在加拿大的家里时，这对夫妇举办了订婚派对。

    engagement /ɛnˈgeɪʤmənt/ 订婚

    012

    The party was in the form of a picnic in a beautiful rose garden.

    聚会的形式是在美丽的玫瑰园野餐。

    in the form of：以……形式 beautiful /ˈbjutəfəl/ 美丽的

    013

    To celebrate and to toast their future, we drank champagne.

    为了庆祝并为他们的未来干杯，我们喝了香槟。

    celebrate /ˈsɛləˌbreɪt/ 庆祝 champagne /ʃæmˈpeɪn/ 香槟酒

    014

    As they posed under an arch covered with climbing roses,we snapped pictures for their photo album.

    当他们在覆盖着攀缘玫瑰的拱门下摆姿势时，我们为他们的相册拍了照片。

    covered /ˈkəvərd/ 覆盖 climbing /ˈklaɪmɪŋ/ 攀登 snapped /snæpt/ 断然地 pictures /ˈpɪkʧərz/ 图片

    015

    The couple decided on a fall wedding.

    这对夫妇决定在秋季举行婚礼。

    decided /ˌdɪˈsaɪdɪd/ 决定 wedding /ˈwɛdɪŋ/ 婚礼

    016

    Plans for the wedding day were activated immediately.

    婚礼当天的计划立即启动。

    wedding /ˈwɛdɪŋ/ 婚礼 activated /ˈæktɪˌveɪtɪd/ 活性 immediately /ˌɪˈmiˌdiətli/ 立即地

    017

    The decisions about the forthcoming event were shared by the whole family.

    关于即将举行的活动的决定由全家人共同决定。

    decisions /dɪˈsɪʒənz/ 决定 forthcoming /ˈfɔrθˈkəmɪŋ/ 即将推出

    018

    A close friend of the family was contacted and invited to sing a solo just prior to the ceremony.

    在仪式前联系了该家庭的一位密友并邀请他独唱。

    prior to：在……之前 contacted /ˈkɑnˌtæktɪd/ 接触过 invited /ˌɪnˈvaɪtɪd/ 邀请 ceremony /ˈsɛrəˌmoʊni/ 仪式

    019

    The day before the ceremony, a rehearsal would take place so that the remainder of the details could be looked after and attended to.

    仪式前一天，将进行一次排练，以便照顾和处理其余的细节。

    take place：发生；举行 ceremony /ˈsɛrəˌmoʊni/ 仪式 rehearsal /rɪˈhərsəl/ 排演 remainder /rɪˈmeɪndər/ 余 details /ˈditeɪlz/ 细节 attended /əˈtɛndəd/ 出席

    020

    The planning that takes place beforehand for one of these events and all the work that the big day entails, with many people collaborating to make the event run smoothly, are immense.

    为其中一项活动提前进行的计划以及这一重要日子所需的所有工作，以及许多人的合作以使活动顺利进行，都是巨大的。

    planning /ˈplænɪŋ/ 规划 beforehand /bɪˈfɔˌrhænd/ 预先 entails /ɪnˈteɪlz/ 需要 collaborating /kəˈlæbərˌeɪtɪŋ/ 合作 smoothly /sˈmuðli/ 顺利

    021

    The conscientious attention to detail will be evident to the spectators of this event.

    对于本次活动的观众来说，对细节的认真关注是显而易见的。

    conscientious /ˌkɑnʃiˈɛnʃəs/ 尽责的 attention /əˈtɛnʃən/ 注意力 evident /ˈɛvədənt/ 明显 spectators /ˈspɛkteɪtərz/ 观众

    022

    I can imagine the excitement and emotions of the family today.

    我可以想象今天一家人的兴奋和激动。

    imagine /ˌɪˈmæʤən/ 想象 excitement /ɪkˈsaɪtmənt/ 激动 emotions /ˈɪˌmoʊʃənz/ 情绪

    023

    The time is near.

    时间快到了。

    024

    The ceremony will begin in 3 short hours.

    仪式将在短短 3 个小时后开始。

    ceremony /ˈsɛrəˌmoʊni/ 仪式

    025

    The bride will be radiant as she comes down the aisle escorted by her father.

    当新娘在父亲的护送下走上红毯时，她会容光焕发。

    bride /braɪd/ 新娘 radiant /ˈreɪˌdiənt/ 容光焕发的 aisle /aɪəl/ 走道 escorted /ɛˈskɔrtɪd/ 护送

    026

    She will wear her grandmother's pearls as an accessory.

    她将佩戴祖母的珍珠作为配饰。

    grandmother /ˈgrændˌməðər/ 祖母 accessory /ækˈsɛsəri/ 配饰

    027

    Her veil will be the same one as her cousin wore last year.

    她的面纱将与她表弟去年戴的面纱相同。

    veil /veɪl/ 面纱

    028

    As is customary, under her garments she will wear a blue garter.

    按照惯例，她的衣服下面会系一条蓝色吊袜带。

    customary /ˈkəstəˌmɛri/ 习惯的 garments /ˈgɑrmənts/ 服装 garter /ˈgɑrtər/ 袜带

    029

    As part of the tradition of Canadian weddings, she will be wearing something old (the pearls), something new (her gown), something borrowed (the veil) and something blue (the garter).

    作为加拿大婚礼传统的一部分，她会穿一些旧的（珍珠）、一些新的（她的礼服）、一些借来的（面纱）和一些蓝色的（吊袜带）。

    tradition /trəˈdɪʃən/ 传统 weddings /ˈwɛdɪŋz/ 婚礼 wearing /ˈwɛrɪŋ/ 穿着 something /ˈsəmθɪŋ/ 某物 gown /gaʊn/ 袍

    030

    As the first chords of the wedding march are played (a melody familiar to all), the congregation will rise.

    当婚礼进行曲的第一和弦响起时（所有人都熟悉的旋律），会众将起立。

    wedding /ˈwɛdɪŋ/ 婚礼 familiar /fəˈmɪljər/ 熟悉的 congregation /ˌkɑŋgrəˈgeɪʃən/ 会众

    031

    Gasps will be heard as they catch a glimpse of the bride in her gorgeous wedding gown.

    当他们看到穿着华丽婚纱的新娘时，会听到喘息声。

    catch a glimpse of：瞥见 bride /braɪd/ 新娘 gorgeous /ˈgɔrʤəs/ 华丽的 wedding /ˈwɛdɪŋ/ 婚礼 gown /gaʊn/ 袍

    032

    The mother of the bride will calmly view this whole event, though tears will blur her vision.

    新娘的母亲会平静地看待这整个事件，尽管泪水会模糊她的视线。

    bride /braɪd/ 新娘 blur /blər/ 模糊

    033

    She will be overcome with sentiment at her daughter's apparent happiness.

    她会因女儿表面上的幸福而感伤不已。

    be overcome with：被某种情绪压倒 sentiment /ˈsɛnəmənt/ 情绪 daughter /ˈdɔtər/ 女儿 apparent /əˈpɛrənt/ 明显的 happiness /ˈhæpinəs/ 幸福

    034

    The picture will be one that will embody hope for the future of humanity.

    这张照片将体现人类未来的希望。

    picture /ˈpɪkʧər/ 图片 humanity /juˈmænɪti/ 人类

    035

    As the couple exchange wedding vows they will be affirming their love and making a commitment to each other.

    当这对夫妇交换结婚誓言时，他们将确认他们的爱并对彼此做出承诺。

    exchange /ɪksˈʧeɪnʤ/ 交换 wedding /ˈwɛdɪŋ/ 婚礼 vows /vaʊz/ 誓言 affirming /əˈfərmɪŋ/ 肯定 commitment /kəˈmɪtmənt/ 承诺

    036

    All the spectators are there to wish them well.

    所有观众都在那里祝愿他们一切顺利。

    wish them well：祝他们一切顺利 spectators /ˈspɛkteɪtərz/ 观众

    037

    As the time approaches, I think back to other family weddings over many years.

    随着时间的临近，我回想起多年来的其他家庭婚礼。

    think back to：回想起 approaches /əˈproʊʧɪz/ 方法 weddings /ˈwɛdɪŋz/ 婚礼

    038

    My older sisters were all married in the same church and as brides they carried a white Bible decorated with tiny roses.

    我的姐姐们都在同一座教堂结婚，作为新娘，她们带着一本装饰着小玫瑰的白色圣经。

    sisters /ˈsɪstərz/ 姐妹 married /ˈmɛrid/ 已婚 carried /ˈkɛrid/ 携带 decorated /ˈdɛkərˌeɪtɪd/ 装饰的

    039

    The receptions for their weddings were all held in the same church basement, as there was no large banquet hall in the local area.

    由于当地没有大型宴会厅，他们的婚礼招待会都在同一个教堂地下室举行。

    receptions /rɪˈsɛpʃənz/ 招待会 weddings /ˈwɛdɪŋz/ 婚礼 basement /ˈbeɪsmənt/ 地下室 banquet /ˈbæŋkwət/ 宴会

    040

    The feast was usually catered by a group of church members.

    宴会通常由一群教会成员主持。

    feast /fist/ 盛宴 usually /ˈjuʒəwəli/ 通常 catered /ˈkeɪtərd/ 迎合 members /ˈmɛmbərz/ 会员

    041

    Today's wedding reception will be held in an arena that will be decorated with just the right amount of fall flowers in wonderful arrays to give it dignity yet a touch of glamour.

    今天的婚宴将在一个场地举行，场地将用适量的秋花装饰，排列精美，既高贵又迷人。

    wedding /ˈwɛdɪŋ/ 婚礼 reception /rɪˈsɛpʃən/ 接待 decorated /ˈdɛkərˌeɪtɪd/ 装饰的 flowers /flaʊərz/ 花朵 wonderful /ˈwəndərfəl/ 精彩的

    042

    The table decorations will be a masterpiece.

    餐桌装饰将是一件杰作。

    decorations /ˌdɛkərˈeɪʃənz/ 装饰 masterpiece /ˈmæstərˌpis/ 杰作

    043

    One of the table decorations will be small pumpkins, used as containers for flowers that were collected from family gardens, surrounded by a large wreath made from long stalks of wheat.

    餐桌装饰之一是小南瓜，用作从家庭花园收集的鲜花的容器，周围环绕着由长麦秆制成的大花环。

    decorations /ˌdɛkərˈeɪʃənz/ 装饰 pumpkins /ˈpəmpkənz/ 南瓜 containers /kənˈteɪnərz/ 容器 flowers /flaʊərz/ 花朵 collected /kəˈlɛktəd/ 集

    044

    There will be a few coloured leaves placed inside the wreath.

    花环内会放置一些彩色叶子。

    coloured /ˈkələrd/ 有色

    045

    Maybe they will use oak and maple leaves to show the splendour of the autumn colours.

    也许他们会用橡树和枫叶来展现秋天的绚丽色彩。

    oak /oʊk/ 橡木 maple /ˈmeɪpəl/ 枫 splendour：辉煌 colours /ˈkələr/ 颜色

    046

    The colour of the outfits that the bridesmaids are wearing will be fall colours as well.

    伴娘所穿服装的颜色也将是秋季颜色。

    outfits /ˈaʊtˌfɪts/ 服装 bridesmaids /ˈbraɪdzˌmeɪdz/ 伴娘 wearing /ˈwɛrɪŋ/ 穿着 colours /ˈkələr/ 颜色

    047

    The ingenious decorations will be appreciated by the many guests attending the festivities.

    巧妙的装饰将受到众多参加庆祝活动的客人的赞赏。

    ingenious /ˌɪnˈʤinjəs/ 巧妙 decorations /ˌdɛkərˈeɪʃənz/ 装饰 appreciated /əˈpriʃiˌeɪtɪd/ 赞赏 attending /əˈtɛndɪŋ/ 出席 festivities /fɛˈstɪvətiz/ 庆祝活动

    048

    At each place setting there will be a special paper napkin with the bride and groom's name printed on it and the date of their wedding.

    每个场所都会有一张特殊的餐巾纸，上面印有新娘和新郎的名字以及婚礼日期。

    setting /ˈsɛtɪŋ/ 环境 special /ˈspɛʃəl/ 特别的 bride /braɪd/ 新娘 printed /ˈprɪnɪd/ 打印 wedding /ˈwɛdɪŋ/ 婚礼

    049

    It will be carefully folded placed on top and of a linen napkin.

    将其小心折叠并放在亚麻餐巾的顶部。

    carefully /ˈkɛrfəli/ 小心

    050

    This will be one of the souvenirs the guests will have to commemorate the special wedding day.

    这将是宾客纪念特别婚礼的纪念品之一。

    souvenirs /ˌsuvəˈnɪrz/ 纪念品 commemorate /kəˈmɛmərˌeɪt/ 纪念 special /ˈspɛʃəl/ 特别的 wedding /ˈwɛdɪŋ/ 婚礼

    051

    Also at each place will be a few after dinner mints for the guests.

    每个地方还会为客人提供一些餐后薄荷糖。

    052

    A miniature tree will also be placed at each person's place.

    每个人的位置还将放置一棵微型树。

    miniature /ˈmɪnɪˌʧʊr/ 微型

    053

    The intent is for the guests to take the little tree home and transplant it to a special place.

    目的是让客人把小树带回家，移植到一个特别的地方。

    transplant /trænˈsplænt/ 移植 special /ˈspɛʃəl/ 特别的

    054

    In this way, our environment is also improved and the tree will become a living tribute to the young couple.

    这样，我们的环境也得到了改善，这棵树也将成为这对年轻夫妇的活祭。

    environment /ɪnˈvaɪrənmənt/ 环境 improved /ˌɪmˈpruvd/ 改进的 tribute /ˈtrɪbjut/ 贡

    055

    The groom will carry her across the threshold as is customary for a young couple as they begin their new life together.

    新郎将抱着她跨过门槛，这是年轻夫妇一起开始新生活的惯例。

    threshold /θˈrɛˌʃoʊld/ 临界点 customary /ˈkəstəˌmɛri/ 习惯的 together /təˈgɛðər/ 一起

    056

    The honeymoon plans will take them to the west coast of Canada.

    蜜月计划将带他们去加拿大西海岸。

    honeymoon /ˈhəniˌmun/ 蜜月 coast /koʊst/ 海岸

    057

    They will have a week of privacy far away from friends and family.

    他们将有一周远离朋友和家人的隐私。

    privacy /ˈpraɪvəsi/ 隐私 friends /frɛndz/ 朋友们

    058

    They will inhabit a condominium in the town of Whistler, British Columbia, in the Rocky Mountains.

    他们将居住在不列颠哥伦比亚省落基山脉惠斯勒镇的一套公寓里。

    inhabit /ˌɪnˈhæbət/ 栖息 condominium /ˌkɑndəˈmɪniəm/ 公寓 columbia /kəˈləmbiə/ 哥伦比亚 mountains /ˈmaʊntənz/ 山

    059

    They will feel as if they are royalty living in a mansion in this little bit of paradise on earth.

    他们会感觉自己就像是住在这座人间天堂的豪宅里的皇室成员。

    royalty /ˈrɔɪəlti/ 版税 mansion /ˈmænʃən/ 大厦 paradise /ˈpɛrəˌdaɪs/ 天堂

    060

    Thereafter, they will return home to Ontario where they will reside.

    此后，他们将返回他们将居住的安大略省。

    thereafter /ðɛˈræftər/ 此后

    061

    Their original plan to spend their honeymoon on board a yacht in the Mediterranean Sea was abandoned when world circumstances dictated that our country Canada was a safer choice.

    当世界形势表明我们国家加拿大是一个更安全的选择时，他们原本计划在地中海的游艇上度蜜月，但他们放弃了。

    original /ərˈɪʤənəl/ 原来的 honeymoon /ˈhəniˌmun/ 蜜月 yacht /jɑt/ 游艇 abandoned /əˈbændənd/ 弃 circumstances /ˈsərkəmˌstænsɪz/ 情况

    062

    Earlier this month, as I thought about the forthcoming marriage, and knew that I would be unable to attend, I decided to write a message to the young couple.

    本月早些时候，当我想到即将举行的婚礼并知道自己无法参加时，我决定给这对年轻夫妇写一封信。

    earlier /ˈərliər/ 早些时候 thought /θɔt/ 想法 forthcoming /ˈfɔrθˈkəmɪŋ/ 即将推出 marriage /ˈmɛrɪʤ/ 婚姻 decided /ˌdɪˈsaɪdɪd/ 决定

    063

    The intent was that the message would be read aloud during the wedding reception.

    目的是在婚宴上大声朗读这条信息。

    message /ˈmɛsɪʤ/ 信息 wedding /ˈwɛdɪŋ/ 婚礼 reception /rɪˈsɛpʃən/ 接待

    064

    In the message, I wished them well and urged them to cherish each other every day.

    在留言中，我祝福他们一切顺利，并嘱咐他们要珍惜彼此每一天。

    message /ˈmɛsɪʤ/ 信息 cherish /ˈʧɛrɪʃ/ 珍惜
